# Design

## Direction
Cartoon-realistic ink wash adventure rendered on a dark lacquer desk. Warm paper pages are the readable surface; indigo night, cinnabar, jade and brass carry the game state.

## Palette
- Ink: `#15171c`
- Night: `#1b2536`
- Paper: `#f0e7d3`
- Cinnabar: `#c94f3d`
- Jade: `#5c9b82`
- Brass: `#d5a85f`

## Typography
Display: `Noto Serif SC`, `Songti SC`, serif. UI: `Inter`, `PingFang SC`, sans-serif.

## Components
Book spread, scene stage, character silhouette, chapter rail, choice button, ending seal.
